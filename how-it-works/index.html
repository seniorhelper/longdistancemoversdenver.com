How the matching works, in plain words

You deserve to know where your phone number goes before you type it in. Here's the whole process.

Who runs this site

Eye To Ad Media, a small Denver marketing company. We are not a moving company or a moving broker: we don't sell moves, set prices, hold deposits or sign contracts with you. We introduce you to movers; the mover you pick is the one you deal with.

Who can receive your request

  • Three movers at most. Never a list of ten.
  • Only checked movers. Before a mover joins, we look up its USDOT number on FMCSA's records and confirm active household-goods operating authority, its legal name and address. We recheck regularly and remove movers whose authority lapses.
  • Only movers that serve your route. Someone moving Denver to Seattle shouldn't hear from a company that only runs to Texas.

We don't show star ratings or "best mover" badges on this site. We can't verify those claims for you, so we'd rather show you how to check movers yourself on the home page.

If no partner covers your move yet

Our partner list is still growing. If no partner mover serves your route, someone from our team will tell you so (by phone or email), point you to the FMCSA lookup, and won't pass your details to anyone else.

How we get paid

Movers in our network pay us for each request they receive, or a monthly fee. You pay nothing, and no mover pays to be shown "first". Movers never pay us based on what you end up spending.

Your choices

  • You're never obliged to hire anyone who calls.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts from that sender, or tell a caller to stop calling.
  • Email zach@eyetoad.com to have your request deleted from our records, and we'll ask partner movers who received it to delete it too.
