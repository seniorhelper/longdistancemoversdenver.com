Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026. This site is operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado ("we").

What we collect

Only what you type into the estimate form: name, phone, optional email, moving-from ZIP code, destination, home size and move month. With it we record the page you used, the date and time, and the exact consent wording you agreed to, so we can prove consent if asked. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data to data brokers.

How we use it

  • To review your request and pass it to up to three moving companies in our partner network that serve your route, so they can contact you with estimates.
  • To contact you ourselves about your request.
  • To keep records needed to prevent spam and meet legal duties.

Partner movers are independent companies with their own privacy practices. We require them to contact you only about your move and to stop when you ask.

Calls and texts

If you tick the consent box, we and up to three partner movers may call or text you about this move, including with automated technology or prerecorded messages. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Reply STOP to opt out of texts; tell any caller to stop calling.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for up to 24 months for records and dispute handling, then delete them. You can ask for earlier deletion.

Your rights

Email zach@eyetoad.com to see, correct or delete your information. Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act where it applies; we honor access, correction and deletion requests from anyone, wherever they live.

Security

The form is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own lead server. Your details never appear in web addresses or analytics.

Children

This site is for adults arranging a move. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.

Long Distance Movers Denver is a free referral service operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado. We are not a moving company.
